Privacy policy

lifelog requests read-only access to the owner's own Gmail (scope gmail.readonly). Email data is processed only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising. No data is collected from anyone other than the owner.

lifelog's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
